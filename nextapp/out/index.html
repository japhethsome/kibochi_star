02About the Company

Connecting Farmers to
Global Coffee Markets

Kibochi Star Coffee Limited is a privately held coffee processor and trader registered in Kenya. The company operates a fully equipped wet mill in Koru, Kisumu County, serving an extensive network of smallholder coffee farmers across Kericho, Kisumu, and Nandi counties.

Our model bridges the gap between the farm gate and the international market providing reliable cherry collection, efficient processing, quality milling, and transparent marketing channels that benefit farmers and buyers alike.

We process both fully washed clean coffee and naturally processed buni coffee, giving buyers a range of origin profiles from the same high altitude growing area.

Kericho

04Our Coffee

Two Distinct Origin Profiles

We produce both fully washed and naturally processed coffee from the same highland farms giving roasters and buyers genuine flexibility without sourcing from multiple origins.

☕

Clean Coffee

Fully Washed

Fully washed Arabica processed at our Koru mill. Cherry is de-pulped, fermented, washed, and graded on raised drying beds — producing a clean, bright cup with clear origin character.

  • Wet-process / fully washed
  • Traceable to pulping station
  • Graded and sorted for export
  • Bright acidity, clean body
🫘

Buni Coffee

Naturally Processed

Naturally processed coffee dried whole in the cherry. The extended fermentation and slow drying develop winey, fruited, and heavier-bodied profiles prized in specialty markets.

  • Natural / dry-process method
  • Dried whole cherry on beds
  • Complex fruited cup profile
  • Sought by specialty buyers
05The Farmer Network

From Farm Gate
to Export Lot

Our collection system is designed around the smallholder farmer — minimising transport distances, ensuring fair cherry pricing, and maintaining traceability at every step.

01

Cherry Delivery at the Pulping Station

Farmers deliver ripe cherry to their nearest of 11 pulping stations across the three counties. Short delivery distances preserve cherry quality and reduce post-harvest losses. Cherry is weighed, recorded, and priced at intake.

02

Processing at the Central Mill in Koru

Collected cherry is transported to the Koru wet mill where it is de-pulped, fermented, washed, and dried. Our mill operates clean water circuits and raised drying beds to produce consistent, export-grade green coffee.

03

Milling, Grading & Market Access

Dried parchment coffee is dry-milled, graded, sorted, and bagged into export lots. Coffee is marketed through local channels and directly to international buyers, with returns traceable back to the originating farmer catchment.
